# User Journeys

Protagonists match the personas in `../ux-numception/EXPERIENCE.md`; UJ-1 to UJ-4 mirror its Flows 1–4.

## Who it is for

- **Kid, about 6–10:** practise arithmetic as play, never boring, never crushing.
- **Adult:** a quick daily mental workout with a clear sense of getting sharper (times, accuracy, personal bests).
- **Both, emotionally:** feel clever; see something you built grow (home tree, constellation, Pyramid); never feel stupid for a mistake.
- **Context:** 15 seconds in a queue or 15 minutes at breakfast; resume exactly; play with no connection.
- **Social (adult, v1+):** compare a result with someone you know.
- **Parent:** hand over the device knowing the child can't chat with strangers, buy things or follow links out.

**Not for (v1):** classrooms and teachers; players wanting a curriculum product with lessons, assessment or reports; competitive real-time multiplayer players; pre-readers below about 5 (onboarding assumes the player reads a short line or has it read aloud).

## UJ-1 Miles (8) finds the second tier [POC]

Can add and subtract, starting multiplication; a parent hands him the phone. Entry: first launch, Kid, Zen, Calm motion.

1. Opens straight into a triangle of numbers: "Find the one that works."
2. After 8 s, "Tap 11" with a ghost finger. He taps 11; the equation is narrated piece by piece.
3. A guided wrong tap: "Try another one", no penalty.
4. After a few guided rounds: "Keep going." Zen rounds follow, each in a new theme.

- **Climax:** first round collapse; the answers fly together into a bigger triangle with no words, and he realises the answers were the puzzle.
- **Resolution:** after about 10 Zen rounds, "New: Fast mode"; he may try Normal or stay in Zen.
- **Edge:** a Zen wrong tap sinks the number like a pebble and it returns; nothing counted; next puzzle quietly easier.
- → FR-7, FR-9, FR-14, FR-18, FR-20, FR-23, FR-25, FR-26, FR-34, FR-41.

## UJ-2 Don challenges his son Chris [Later]

Grown-up profile. Menu → Challenge → plays a seeded round → shares the seed link via the OS share sheet (puzzle content only). Chris plays solo and shares his time back by hand. Don wins by 4 s; both have the round in stats. **Edge:** in a Kid profile the Challenge entry does not exist. → FR-5, FR-36.

## UJ-3 Don's Normal session [POC]

Resumes mid-round in Normal, Grown-up.

1. The chain window drains around the shape outline and refills on each solve.
2. Combo heat climbs 1x→2x; lines glow wider.
3. One wrong tap: power cut, combo to 1x, no points lost, play continues.
4. Round collapses; the round-end flash shows time, points, best combo.

- **Climax:** beats his best round time.
- **Resolution:** closes mid-round; next launch resumes exactly there.
- → FR-9, FR-10, FR-15–FR-17, FR-19, FR-34.

## UJ-4 A Tower round [POC]

Don, n=3. 9 tier-1 puzzles collapse into 3 tier-2, which collapse into 1 tier-3; the camera pulls further out at each collapse. **Climax:** the tier-3 solve with the whole tower visible for a beat. **Resolution:** a theme transition into the next round. → FR-2, FR-11.

## UJ-5 Miles keeps his streak and grows his tree [v1]

Fourth day in a row, 2 minutes before school. The app opens to his home tree and glides to his next unsolved puzzle.

- Solving one puzzle brightens his streak light in the structure, not as a number; a few more push the fog back.
- **Climax:** a never-seen glyph (×) in an easy first puzzle (3×3=9), then narrated.
- **Resolution:** puts the phone down mid-tier; nothing lost; nothing nags him back.
- **Edge:** missed yesterday with an earned freeze → streak survives quietly; with none → restarts with no loss message.
- → FR-7, FR-12, FR-24, FR-28, FR-29.

## UJ-6 Don's Daily Seed [v1]

Opens the Daily Seed → plays the fixed round → a share card shows his result coloured by speed → shares via the OS share sheet into a family chat. **Climax:** his card beats his sister's. **Resolution:** a new seed tomorrow. **Edge:** in a Kid profile the card is image-only. → FR-32, FR-33.
