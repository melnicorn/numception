# Digest: Kakooma — origin and IP status (round 1, digest 1)

Accessed: 2026-10-08. Not legal advice. Facts only, with sources.

**Database access flag:** I could NOT query the trademark databases directly. tmsearch.uspto.gov returned only a JS shell with no results. trademarks.justia.com returned 403. I did not reach WIPO Global Brand Database or EUIPO (both are JS apps; not attempted within budget). As a result, no registration number, class, or owner record for KAKOOMA was verified.

## Findings

1. **claim:** Greg Tang created Kakooma. In a post dated 2011-12-09, Boston Partners in Education describes Kakooma as "created by" Greg Tang: find the number in a group that is the sum of two others. Tang was then selling a Common Core-aligned online subscription.
   - source: https://bostonpartners.org/math-a-whole-new-way/ | publisher: Boston Partners in Education | pub_date: 2011-12-09 | confidence: high (creator), medium (date of origin, which is "by 2011") | class: origin

2. **claim:** The iOS App Store listing "Kakooma: Math Brain Game" (id1589291968) names the developer as Tang Math Games, LLC. It says the game was "invented by Harvard mathematician Greg Tang" and that the name comes from the Spanish "cacumen" (keen insight). The listing writes the name with ®. The direct fetch returned 404 on 2026-10-08, so the app may have been delisted or moved. The details come from a search-engine snippet.
   - source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kakooma-math-brain-game/id1589291968 | publisher: Apple App Store (developer Tang Math Games, LLC) | pub_date: unknown | confidence: medium | class: origin / existing apps

3. **claim:** Tang Math (tangmath.com) publishes a "Kakooma Legacy" page with Kakooma® Plus, Negatives, Times and Fractions variants, from 4x4 up to 9x9 groups. The footer reads "Copyright © 2026 Tang Math." The page does not name the creator.
   - source: https://tangmath.com/Kakoomalegacy | publisher: Tang Math | pub_date: 2026 (footer) | confidence: high | class: publication / trademark claim

4. **claim:** Tang Math Games sells physical card products such as "Kakooma® Plus" (grades 1–5, ASIN B06XT4PT5Z), marked ® and "© 2025 Tang Math Games." Store pages (store.tangmath.com, Amazon brand store, Teachers Pay Teachers "Kakooma Addition Laminates Bundle by Greg Tang Math") show sales across several channels.
   - source: https://tangmathgames.com/products/kakooma-plus-1-pack ; https://store.tangmath.com/products/kakooma-times-20-pack ; https://www.amazon.com/stores/TangMathGames/page/149E475D-C536-4510-B74F-754E07FDC279 ; https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Kakooma-Addition-Laminates-Bundle-2573981 | publisher: Tang Math Games / Amazon / TpT | pub_date: 2025 | confidence: high | class: publication / trademark claim

5. **claim:** The owner consistently uses the ® symbol on "Kakooma" across its own sites. Using ® is lawful only for a federally registered mark, which strongly suggests a registration exists. I could not confirm a registration number, class, or status.
   - source: findings 3–4 above | confidence: medium (inference) | class: trademark

6. **claim:** A second, apparently unrelated iOS app, "Kakooma: Math Brain Puzzles" (App Store id6746321576), was reportedly released 2025-07-09 and updated 2026-01-05. It is free, with in-app purchases up to $49.99. The developer was not identified, and direct fetch was refused (403). This suggests a third party already uses the name. Whether that use is licensed or has been challenged is unknown.
   - source: https://appshunter.io/ios/app/kakooma-math-brain-puzzles/id6746321576 (via search snippet) | publisher: AppsHunter (aggregator) | pub_date: 2026-01 | confidence: low-medium | class: existing apps

7. **claim:** The US Copyright Office's FL-108 factsheet says copyright does not protect the idea for a game, its name or title, or its method of play. Once a game is public, others may develop games "based on similar principles." Rules text and artwork can be registered.
   - source: https://webharvest.gov/peth04/20041015023438/http://www.copyright.gov/fls/fl108.html | publisher: U.S. Copyright Office (archived 2004 copy) | pub_date: ≤2004 | confidence: high (principle); I did not fetch the current copyright.gov version | class: legal principle

8. **claim:** Under 17 U.S.C. §102(b), game rules, mechanics and other functional elements are not copyrightable. Protection covers the specific expression (art, text, audiovisual presentation), and patents are the main route for protecting mechanics.
   - source: https://www.americanbar.org/groups/intellectual_property_law/resources/landslide/archive/why-videogame-rules-are-not-expression-protected-copyright-law/ | publisher: ABA Landslide | pub_date: 2015 (Mar/Apr) | confidence: high | class: legal principle

## Leads
- Query USPTO Trademark Search manually for "KAKOOMA" (word mark) and owner "Tang Math" / "Greg Tang". Likely classes are 28 (card games), 9 (apps/software), 16 (printed books) and 41 (educational services).
- Check the WIPO Global Brand Database and EUIPO eSearch for any foreign filings.
- Check whether Tang holds any patent on the puzzle format (Google Patents: inventor "Gregory Tang"). Patents are the only route to protecting mechanics.
- Look for Greg Tang books that contain Kakooma (e.g. "Kakooma" puzzle books, Scholastic titles) and their publication dates, for an earlier origin date.
- Identify the developer of App Store id6746321576 and check whether Tang has challenged it.
- Search Google Play directly for "Kakooma".
- Try gregtangmath.com (the www host had a TLS certificate error, so use the bare domain).
