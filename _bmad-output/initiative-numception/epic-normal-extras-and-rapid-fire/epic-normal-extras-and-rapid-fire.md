# Overdrive, graded cooling, clean bonus and Rapid Fire

## Description

Normal gains Overdrive, graded combo cooling and the clean-round bonus, and Rapid Fire arrives with earn-only totems. v1 scope: FR-13, FR-50 to FR-52.

## Outcome

Adults get a short, intense alternative and deeper Normal play; the signal is SM-9 Normal adoption.

## Done when

1. Overdrive doubles top-puzzle points and a power cut cancels it.
2. An empty chain window drops combo one step; only a power cut resets it.
3. A clean round earns the tuning bonus, soft misses included.
4. Rapid Fire's death-and-restart takes under 1 s and a totem absorbs one error per run; totems are never sold.
5. Live in production on iOS, Android and web.

## Boundaries

Normal and Rapid Fire rules only.
