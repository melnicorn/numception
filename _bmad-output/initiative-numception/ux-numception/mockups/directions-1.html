Numception: visual directions, round 1

Three Normal and three Zen personalities on the same n=3 tier-2 scene. The camera is zoomed onto the active tier-1 cell (8, 5, 13 with +, so 8 + 5 = 13), with the solved cell (7) lower-left, the locked cell lower-right and the tier-2 operator in the centre. Each small frame shows a wrong tap on 5. Animations are off when reduced motion is set.

Normal

Vector Noir

A pure-black void with a faint Tron floor receding below the puzzle; everything is drawn in 1–1.5 px hairlines that only become light through a tight double-blur glow. Numbers are Avenir Next Ultra Light with open tracking, so the scene reads like a vector display: precise, cold, very legible. Solved cells keep their edge and take a 9% hue wash; locked cells dissolve into a dotted hairline over fog.

  • #000000 void
  • #00A3FF neon @1x
  • #4FE0FA neon @1.6x
  • #7CFFF2 neon @2x
  • #E8FDFF numerals
  • #FF1E3C error
  • #4A0711 error fill
Normal

Overdrive Bloom

Heavy 5 px neon tubes with rounded joins, each cell its own segmented tube separated by a dark gap, and a big two-stage bloom that visibly swells as combo climbs. Futura Bold numerals feel loud and arcade-confident. Solved cells become solid lit plates with the answer knocked out in the background colour; locked cells are a faint diagonal hatch.

  • #1D0B33 bg core
  • #7B4DFF neon @1x
  • #D646EC neon @1.6x
  • #FF3FD0 neon @2x
  • #FFB8F4 timer core

  • #FF2340 error
  • #3D0410 error fill
Normal

Obsidian Facet

Depth instead of bloom: every cell is a shallow faceted pyramid in near-black stone, and the neon is only a rim light on the facet edges (closer to Monument Valley than Tron). DIN Alternate tabular numerals feel engineered and instrument-like; faint scanlines give the background texture. Solved cells light their facets from inside; locked cells stay as unlit stone with static.

  • #11171A bg top
  • #040506 bg bottom
  • #20292C facet
  • #2BFF88 neon @1x
  • #A9FF55 neon @1.6x
  • #F2FF3A neon @2x
  • #F1FFE4 numerals
  • #FF3B30 error
  • #3A0806 error fill
Zen

Washi Ink

The literal “paper + one ink” idea: warm washi with a faint fibre grain, single indigo ink drawn as slightly wavering brush hairlines. Avenir Next Regular numerals sit quietly. A solved cell gets a soft ink wash bleeding inward; locked cells are lines fading into mist. A wrong tap simply sinks into the paper with a faint ripple.

  • #F2EDE3 paper
  • #283A6B indigo ink
  • #283A6B @13% solved wash
Zen

Moonwater

Exploring around “paper”: a dim, low-contrast night pond in slate teal with one moonlight ink. Soft 2.5 px rounded strokes at half opacity with a gentle halo; rounded system numerals. Solved cells hold a pool of moonlight; locked cells are under deep fog. Calm, bedtime-friendly, still not black-and-neon.

  • #16242C bg top
  • #223943 bg bottom
  • #BFE3DA moonlight ink
Zen

Kiln

No lines at all: cells are clay planes separated by gaps, raised with a soft warm shadow, like tiles laid on a table. One terracotta ink on sand. Futura Medium numerals. Solving fires a tile, which turns solid terracotta with a pale number; locked tiles sit sunken and unglazed. A wrong tap presses the tile into the clay and the number sinks.

  • #ECE3D7 sand top
  • #DCCDBB sand bottom
  • #F6F0E8 tile
  • #D6C6B2 locked tile
  • #A14E36 terracotta ink
