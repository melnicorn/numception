Numception shape candidates, n = 3–9

Candidate cell layouts for each puzzle size n, drawn in neutral line style so only geometry is being compared. Each candidate shows (a) a tier-1 puzzle with real numbers and the operator in the centre (every sample has exactly one valid triple), (b) the tier-2 view where each number cell holds a miniature of a tier-1 puzzle and solved ones collapse to their bold answer, and (c) the tier-1 puzzle in a 375×667 phone-portrait frame at real size, with dashed lines marking the 16px gutters and numbers at 20px or more. The notes record observations about the centre operator space and how well the cell shape nests the whole shape. Nothing has been chosen yet.
