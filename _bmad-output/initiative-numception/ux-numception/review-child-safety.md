# Numception — Child-safety & Privacy Review

## Verdict

The play experience itself is unusually child-safe (no ads, no chat, no usernames, no loot boxes, no red or penalties in Zen, positive-only kid stats), but the protective layer around it is not yet specified well enough to ship to an audience that includes 8-year-olds. The single kid/adult toggle is the only gate in front of every adult feature, no default profile is stated, and nothing stops a child from flipping it. Several v1 features also escape the "social entries absent in kid profile" rule because they are not labelled social: Game Center / Play Games leaderboards, the Daily Seed share card, and receiving a challenge link. The spine also treats third-party SDK limits as per-profile, when App Store Kids Category and Google Play Families rules apply to the whole app binary. None of this needs the core design to change. Before PRD, it does need explicit defaults (kid-first, data on-device), a profile-gating rule that covers every outward-facing surface, a parent gate in front of purchases and external links, and the legal and policy research already flagged in the memlog.

## Findings

1. **[critical]** No default profile is stated, and first run cannot ask (EXPERIENCE → Foundation "no menus on launch"; State Patterns "First run"; Profiles & Child Safety). First run opens straight into play with no interruption, so whatever the toggle defaults to is what every child gets. AADC (high privacy by default) and COPPA (actual knowledge, mixed audience) both point to a protective default. *Fix:* state that the app starts in kid profile and stays there until an adult deliberately switches behind a gate. Treat "unverified" and "kid" as the same profile everywhere. Add a State Patterns row for this.

2. **[critical]** A child can flip the kid/adult toggle (EXPERIENCE → Component Patterns `control-surface`; Profiles "Beta: simple kid / adult toggle"). It sits in the same panel as effects and theme settings, one tap from play. An 8-year-old will find it. In the docs, "verified adult" means nothing more than this toggle. *Fix:* even in beta, put kid → adult behind a parent gate: an adult-knowledge or multi-step gate, not "type the year you were born" and not simple arithmetic, since solving maths is literally this child's skill. Never gate adult → kid. Define "verified adult" explicitly. Until the Later age-signal work lands, it means "passed the parent gate", and that gate opens only the features listed in finding 6.

3. **[critical]** Third-party SDK rule is scoped per profile (EXPERIENCE → Profiles "no third-party ad/tracking libraries in kid profiles"). In Apple's Kids Category and Google Play's Families programme, the restrictions on third-party analytics and advertising apply to the app, not to a runtime profile. If the binary ships an analytics or crash SDK that sends data for adult sessions, the whole app is exposed. On web, the same applies to any analytics script. *Fix:* rewrite as "no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs in the app, all platforms". If telemetry is needed, it must be first-party, on-device by default, aggregate and identifier-free, with crash reporting limited to what the stores permit. Record the Kids Category vs general-audience listing decision (see research list).

4. **[high]** Leaderboards are not covered by the kid-profile rule (EXPERIENCE → Responsive & Platform "Game Center / Play Games leaderboards (v1)"; State Patterns "Kid profile: social and challenge entries absent"). Leaderboards show other players' aliases and avatars, and they link to platform friend and profile features. That is a contact and identity vector the app does not control. They are also a comparison surface, which contradicts the kid voice rule "no comparisons for kids". *Fix:* make leaderboards and platform sign-in adult-profile-only. Never trigger a Game Center or Play Games sign-in prompt for a kid profile. Classify leaderboards explicitly as a "social" entry.

5. **[high]** The Daily Seed share card is not profile-gated (EXPERIENCE → Information Architecture "Daily Seed card | v1 | Mode; share"). The share sheet hands content to Messages, WhatsApp, social apps and so on. A child-initiated share is app-originated outbound contact, and the card may carry time, score or identity. *Fix:* in kid profile, either remove share or limit it to an image-only card with no link, no name, no account or device identifier and no install attribution token. Add a State Patterns row for it.

6. **[high]** Receiving a challenge or seed link is unspecified (EXPERIENCE → Flow 2; Seeds; Open Q 7). The rules only cover sending. A child's device can receive Don's link, and anyone can send a child a link. What the recipient sees (sender name? message?), and whether a result goes back to the sender, decides whether this is a contact channel. *Fix:* a deep link carries puzzle content only (numbers and operators, optionally a layout seed). It carries no sender identity, no free text, no return address and no tracking parameters. In a kid profile an incoming link just opens the puzzle as solo play, with no "reply" or "send result" affordance. "Chris played" must not reach Don through a server-side link between two identities in a kid context (see 7).

7. **[high]** The result-notification mechanism implies accounts and a contact graph (EXPERIENCE → Open Q 7b; memlog "result notification undecided"). Any option that tells Don "Chris played" means storing a relationship between two people's devices or accounts. That creates personal data and a contact vector. Push also needs a device token. *Fix:* pick the share-back option as the default: the recipient manually shares their result card back through their own share sheet, and nothing is stored server-side. If push is ever added, it is adult-profile-only and off by default, and no push token is requested in a kid profile.

8. **[high]** No parent gate before purchase (EXPERIENCE → Inspiration & Anti-patterns "flat one-time purchase"; DESIGN → Theme packs "unlock unit"). The purchase entry point is not placed anywhere. Kids Category requires a parental gate before purchase and before external links, and that includes rate-this-app and support/website links. *Fix:* in kid profile, put every purchase, external link and store review prompt behind the parent gate. Never show the purchase prompt in play or in a transition.

9. **[high]** Shared family device (EXPERIENCE → Foundation "launch straight into the next unsolved puzzle"; Profiles). This is one device with one global toggle. If Don leaves it in adult mode, Miles picks it up and gets challenges, leaderboards and share with no visible cue. Stats from both players merge into one record, so Miles's "You solved 42" includes Dad's solves, and Don's adult stats show Miles's errors. *Fix:* decide between (a) multiple local profiles with a picker behind the menu, never on launch, or (b) the adult profile auto-reverting to kid after the app has been backgrounded for some time. Either way, show a subtle persistent cue for adult mode in the control surface, and keep stats per profile.

10. **[medium]** "Stats captured identically for kids and adults" (memlog; EXPERIENCE → `stats-panel`). Error counts, accuracy, response times and adaptive-difficulty state together make a learning profile of a child. Data minimisation and AADC profiling-off-by-default ask for a reason for each field and for knowing where it lives. Storage location (device vs backend) is not stated. *Fix:* state "stats stay on-device; no stats leave the device for a kid profile". Keep only what the kid or adult views and the difficulty engine need, and say so in the spine. Treat adaptive difficulty as core-service processing that runs on-device and is never exported or used for any other purpose.

11. **[medium]** Engagement mechanics read as streak pressure for kids (EXPERIENCE → Component Patterns `combo-glow` "unbroken round → Overdrive"; Soft miss "does not break the streak"; IA "Daily Seed"; "Pack collection … undiscovered slots"; Rhythm "playable … 15 minutes", infinite play, no end screen). There are no streak threats in the copy, which is good. However, daily seeds, visible empty collection slots and infinite play with no natural stopping point are the patterns AADC standard 13 (nudge techniques) and loss-aversion guidance warn about. They have a stronger pull when the player is 8 and the content may be paid. *Fix:* for kid profile, show no "you missed yesterday's seed" state, no daily-streak counters, and no locked slots that tease paid content (show only what is owned). Give a natural session pause, for example a calm "nice place to stop" beat after N rounds that never blocks play. Make sure "streak" exists only as in-round combo, never across days.

12. **[medium]** Normal unlock for kids (EXPERIENCE → IA "Normal unlock moment"; Modes). After about 10 rounds a timed mode with a red power cut and a thud becomes available. The docs do not say whether the switch is automatic. *Fix:* the unlock is an offer, never an automatic switch. In kid profile, Zen stays the default mode and Normal is opt-in each session or until changed. Consider softening `error` and the thud for kid profile.

13. **[medium]** Web build has no consent or storage story (EXPERIENCE → Foundation "web-first"). On the open web there are no OS age signals, local storage is shared by anyone using the browser, and hosting or CDN logs can capture IP addresses. *Fix:* the web build uses no cookies beyond strictly necessary ones, no analytics scripts and no third-party fonts or CDNs that log the user (self-host fonts; see DESIGN Open Q 15 on webfonts). The same kid-first default applies.

14. **[medium]** No transparency or controls surface for parents (EXPERIENCE → Control surface lists mode, settings, stats, toggle only). AADC and COPPA both expect clear, age-appropriate privacy information and a way to review and delete data. *Fix:* add a short "For grown-ups" entry behind the parent gate. It states what is stored, where and why, and offers "reset progress / delete all data". Give a kid-readable one-liner in the kid view ("Your progress stays on this device").

15. **[low]** Geolocation, camera, microphone and contacts are not stated as off (all docs). *Fix:* add one line to Profiles & Child Safety saying the app requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone or tracking permission (ATT) on any platform. Daily Seed date uses the device clock or timezone only, with no location lookup.

16. **[low]** There is no rule against free text or usernames (EXPERIENCE → Profiles). Nothing exists today, which is good, but nothing prevents a later "name your profile" or "name your playlist" that ends up in share cards. *Fix:* add a rule: no free-text fields that can leave the device; local profile names (if 9a) are never included in shares, links or leaderboards.

17. **[low]** Teacher / smart-board mode (EXPERIENCE → Responsive & Platform "Later"). School use brings school-consent rules and FERPA-style obligations. *Fix:* keep it offline and accountless, with no class rosters, until reviewed.

18. **[low]** Kid stats wording guards against negatives but not against comparisons with self over time that could discourage, for example "slower than last week" (EXPERIENCE → Voice and Tone). *Fix:* extend the kid rule to "growth only, never decline", including speed.

## Strengths worth keeping

No in-app messaging, no usernames, no ads or interstitials, no loot boxes or currencies, positive-only kid stats, no red or penalty in Zen or onboarding, "Kid profile: entries absent (not disabled)", full offline play, effects-only sound with the mute switch respected, reduced-motion fallback. These are a strong base for a Kids Category or Families submission.

## Needs legal/policy research

- **COPPA:** whether the app is "directed to children" or mixed-audience. What counts as actual knowledge given a kid toggle. Whether any persistent identifier (push token, Game Center ID, crash-report device ID, analytics ID) is collected for kids and under which exception. Verifiable parental consent if any data leaves the device.
- **GDPR / GDPR-K and UK AADC:** age of digital consent per EU member state (13–16). Whether on-device adaptive difficulty counts as profiling. Whether a DPIA is required. The AADC 15 standards checklist, especially defaults, nudges, parental controls transparency and geolocation.
- **Apple App Store:** Kids Category eligibility (guidelines 1.3 and 5.1.4) vs a general listing with a 4+ rating. Whether Game Center leaderboards are acceptable for a kid audience. Parental-gate requirements for purchases and external links. Behaviour of the Declared Age Range API and how far it can be relied on for "infer age from system".
- **Google Play:** Families policy and the self-certified SDK list. Target-audience declaration consequences. Play Age Signals API (where available). Play Games Services for under-13 users.
- **Share links and deep links:** whether an app-initiated share by a child counts as "disclosure" under COPPA. Universal / App Link attribution parameters.
- **Push notifications to minors:** platform and regulatory position if result notifications ever ship.
- **Web build:** ePrivacy / cookie rules for a child-directed site. US state laws (for example California AADC and other state minor-privacy laws, check current enforcement status).
- **Schools (Later):** COPPA school-authorisation exception, FERPA, and EU/UK school procurement norms for a teacher mode.
- **Parent-gate design:** current accepted patterns, and whether a maths-based gate is acceptable for a maths game (likely not).
