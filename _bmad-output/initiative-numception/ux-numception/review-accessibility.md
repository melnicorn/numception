# Accessibility review — Numception

**Verdict: not shippable as specified.** The spines have a good Accessibility Floor, but the theme tables break it. **Kiln** is the first-run theme and the only screen Miles sees on day one. Its cell boundaries sit at 1.12:1. Its narration highlight is the same hex as the numbers it highlights, and its one control fails 3:1. In Normal, a wrong tap does several bad things at once. It causes a red pulse and a flicker with no frequency limit, and the next tap is accepted at once, so a child mashing the screen can set off repeated red flashes. It dims every numeral to 2.4–2.9:1 in two of three themes. It shrinks the timer to 1.5:1 while the timer keeps running. Motion safety hangs entirely on an OS setting that almost no child's device has turned on, while zoom is the game's core way of moving around. The play view is canvas-only, with no plan for screen readers, so even the menu that holds the motion setting may not be reachable. Most of this is fixable at the token and spec level before any code exists. Fix it now.

Severity counts: **3 critical, 8 high, 8 medium, 4 low.**

## Findings

**[critical]** Power-cut flashing has no frequency limit, and children mash buttons (DESIGN.md → Components → Power cut; EXPERIENCE.md → Component Patterns → Power cut, State Patterns → Error). A wrong tap gives an `{colors.error}` pulse (#FF1E3C, #FF2340, #FF3B30, each 5.1–5.5:1 against near-black) and "lines flicker dark". Every numeral drops to dim, and "next tap accepted immediately after the flash". None of the following is specified: how fast the flicker runs, how many pulses there are, or a cooldown. A frustrated 8-year-old tapping 4–6 times a second will set off 4–6 red flashes a second over a cell that fills a large share of the screen at n=3 hero scale. That is exactly what WCAG 2.3.1 treats as a red flash, at its most dangerous. Rapid Fire's "death animation is the restart" (v1) adds the same risk. *Fix:* Make the whole power cut a single event with at most 1 luminance flip, and no more than 3 flashes in any 1 s window across all sources. Coalesce wrong taps inside a 600–800 ms window into one cut: count them, but don't replay the effect. Replace "flicker" with a monotonic fade to dark. Keep the red to the cell edge only, never a filled red area. Add a design rule that no effect may alternate luminance more than 3 times a second.

**[critical]** Kiln, the first-run theme, has no visible cell boundaries (DESIGN.md → Zen themes, Kiln column; `line`: "none (gaps between tiles)"). The shape is read only through tile #F6F0E8 against sand #ECE3D7, which is **1.12:1**, plus a "soft warm shadow" with no value given. Locked tiles reach only 1.47:1 against open tiles. WCAG 1.4.11 asks for 3:1 for the graphics needed to understand the content. For Miles, "which shapes are cells, and which belong together" *is* the content. This is the first screen he ever sees. *Fix:* Give Kiln a real `line` (an ink edge or rim at ≥3:1 against both tile and surface), or darken the surface until tile vs surface is ≥3:1. Specify the shadow token and check it. Locked tiles need a non-colour cue: an inset texture, or a dashed rim at ≥3:1.

**[critical]** In the themes where narration happens, equation narration highlights in the same colour as the numbers (DESIGN.md → `equation-narration.highlight: {colors.accent}`; Kiln `accent` = `number` = **#A14E36**; Washi `accent` = `number` = **#283A6B**). The only teaching mechanism ("7 … + … 4 … = … 11, each part lighting in accent", EXPERIENCE.md Flow 1 step 3) changes nothing visible on the first-run theme. It fails every player, not just disabled ones. *Fix:* Make the highlight a non-colour change: scale the term up (for example 1.25×), lift the tile, swap to filled (as `cell-solved`), or underline it. Add a narration voice-over track (it also serves pre-readers and blind players). Add a grammar rule: any "highlight" role must differ from the element it highlights by ≥3:1 *or* change shape or size.

**[high]** Power cut makes the board unreadable while play continues (DESIGN.md → Normal themes, `number-dimmed`). Every numeral drops to 2.91:1 (Overdrive Bloom) or **2.38:1** (Obsidian Facet, on the facet the numbers actually sit on). The spine's "2.9:1 (intended)" for Obsidian is measured against the bare surface, not the facet, so the doc understates the miss. EXPERIENCE.md says "play never stops; next tap accepted immediately". So the player has to choose the next answer from numbers they can't read. Low-vision players and children in sunlight are locked out until the reboot, and the reboot has no stated duration. *Fix:* Dim the glow and lines, not the numerals. Or set `number-dimmed` to ≥4.5:1 in every theme (Obsidian needs about #8A9A8E or lighter on #20292C), and cap the dim state at ≤500 ms. Correct the contrast table.

**[high]** The Normal timer vanishes on a power cut, has no expiry rule, and isn't adjustable (DESIGN.md → `timer-line-dimmed`; EXPERIENCE.md Open Q21, Component Patterns → Menu button "[ASSUMPTION]"). The dimmed timer measures 1.52:1 (Overdrive), 1.50:1 (Obsidian) and 2.93:1 (Vector Noir) against its track. Time information disappears right when the player is under the most stress, and the timer is a non-text graphic that needs 3:1. What happens at expiry is undefined. Pausing via the menu is only an assumption. There is no extend or relax option, so WCAG 2.2.1 rests entirely on "Zen exists". That's arguable for adults, but Normal unlocks automatically for an 8-year-old. *Fix:* `timer-line-dimmed` ≥3:1 against its track in every theme. Decide expiry as non-punitive (for example, the line just turns over and costs the combo, never the round). Make the menu pause the timer (a decision, not an assumption). Add a "relaxed timer" setting (1.5×, 2×, off), default relaxed in kid profiles.

**[high]** The soft miss is colour-only and looks like a wrong tap (DESIGN.md `soft-miss` = `operator` tone; EXPERIENCE.md State Patterns → Soft miss). In Kiln, soft-miss #BB7F6B against the normal number is 1.74:1, and against Zen `number-sunk` #CBA491 it is about 1.4:1. The cue that's meant to teach "you found a valid relation, but tap the *result*" can't be told apart from "wrong", and it takes on the operator's colour. That breaks 1.4.1 (use of colour), and the pedagogy goes with it. *Fix:* Give soft miss its own non-colour behaviour. For example, the tapped cell pulses once in place without sinking, and a ghost arrow or glow travels to the correct result cell. Say "Tap the answer" once. Mock it before PRD.

**[high]** Vestibular risk rests on one OS switch (EXPERIENCE.md → Accessibility Floor "honour OS setting"; Interaction Primitives → Zoom "Powers of Ten"; DESIGN.md → Zoom-hide "camera accelerates into the solved cell", Ripple "refract", Crystal fracture "tilt, spin… parallax"). Zoom isn't decoration here. It is how the player moves between tiers, and it fires on every solve, every 10–30 s. Accelerating dive-ins and full-screen displacement are textbook vestibular triggers. Almost no child's device has Reduce Motion on, and a parent won't know to set it. *Fix:* Add an in-app motion setting with three levels: Full, Calm (no camera acceleration, no displacement or parallax; zoom becomes a ≤300 ms scale with a crossfade), and Minimal. Show it once during first-run onboarding or on a parent screen, and make kid profiles default to Calm. Cap zoom scale velocity, and never accelerate a dive. Under reduced motion, keep orientation with a static "you are here" mini-map, so the tier model isn't lost when zooms become cuts.

**[high]** The canvas-only UI shuts out screen readers, Switch Control and keyboard, including the settings that fix the other problems (EXPERIENCE.md → Foundation "UI system: none; custom-drawn"; Open Q22; DESIGN.md → control surface and stats "layout not designed"). If the control surface is drawn on canvas, VoiceOver and TalkBack users can't reach the mode switch, the effects/motion/haptics toggles, or the kid/adult toggle. The web build fails 2.1.1 Keyboard and 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value. *Fix:* Decide now that the control surface, stats and onboarding text are DOM (or native) elements laid over the canvas, with real labels and focus order. For the play view, keep a parallel accessible tree: each cell is a button labelled with its number and state ("7, open", "11, solved", "locked"), the operator is announced, the equation narration goes to an aria-live region, and the menu is reachable by keyboard. Arrow keys plus Enter on web.

**[high]** The onboarding copy doesn't explain the game to an 8-year-old (EXPERIENCE.md → Voice and Tone; Flow 1; Open Q16). "Pick the only solution" assumes Miles already knows there is an equation hidden among the cells. "Solution" and "only" are abstract, and the alternative, "One number from the others", isn't a sentence a child can act on. The mechanic gets explained only through the hint ("Tap 11") and the narration after the fact, and the narration is invisible in Kiln (see critical finding). *Fix:* Write for a Year 3 reading level with concrete words: "Two numbers make another. Tap the answer." Test it with real 7–9-year-olds. Add optional spoken guide lines (they help pre-readers, dyslexic players and blind players). Show a ghost-finger demo on round 1 before asking anything.

**[high]** The one visible control fails non-text contrast in the first-run theme (DESIGN.md → `menu-button.color: {colors.operator}`). Kiln operator on sand is **2.60:1**, below the 3:1 that WCAG 1.4.11 sets for UI components. The glyph and position are undesigned, so there is no shape to make up for it. *Fix:* Draw the menu button in `{colors.number}` (4.52:1 on sand) or on a tile. Give it a visible 44×44pt or larger hit area, and specify the glyph (a conventional ≡ or ⋯, not an abstract mark).

**[high]** Hairline numerals in Vector Noir (DESIGN.md → Typography: Avenir Next Ultra Light, weight 200, +0.06em, under a σ 2.4 + 7 blur). The 19.9:1 figure is meaningless when strokes are about 1 px. At the 20 px floor (n=7–9) and with bloom on top, stroke contrast collapses, and 1/7 and 3/8 blur together. Washi and Moonwater operators at weight 300 have the same, milder, problem. *Fix:* Set a minimum numeral weight per size: ≥400 below 32 px and ≥300 at hero size. Keep glow off the numerals themselves (glow the lines only). Check the legibility of 1/7, 3/8 and 6/9 at 20 px for each face.

**[medium]** Operator contrast is treated as "decorative-plus", but the operator is half the equation (DESIGN.md → Number contrast floor "operator… may sit lower but not below 3:1"). Kiln is 2.92:1 on tile and 2.60:1 where a carved centre sits on sand. Washi is 3.28:1 and Moonwater 3.41:1 on `surface-deep`. Mixing up × and + on an equation puzzle is a correctness failure, not a cosmetic one. *Fix:* Hold operators to 4.5:1 like numbers. Kiln needs about #9A4A33 or darker on tile, so derive operator from accent at ≥90%, not 70%.

**[medium]** Colour-vision collisions in Normal (DESIGN.md → Normal themes; combo ramps). Overdrive Bloom's `error` #FF2340 sits next to a magenta accent and solid magenta solved plates #D646EC. Under deutan simulation their luminance ratio is 1.05:1, under protan 1.44:1, and even typical vision sees red and magenta as close. Under deutan, Obsidian's error turns olive (#A99725) and its lime accent turns yellow (#FDE664), so both read as yellow. Combo ramps collapse under protan: Overdrive 1x vs 2x is 1.06:1, Obsidian 1.05:1. The spine's claim that combo is "colour plus width and bloom" is the only thing carrying the ramp, and width and bloom have no stated values. *Fix:* Give the error cell a non-colour mark that lasts past the pulse, such as a cracked or broken edge or an X-notch, held until the next tap. Specify combo line widths per step (for example 1 / 1.75 / 2.5 px) and bloom radii as tokens. Run every theme through a CVD check as a grammar requirement.

**[medium]** Solved and locked states depend on low-contrast fills (State Patterns; DESIGN.md theme tables). Solved fill against its surround is 1.12 (Vector Noir), 1.24 (Washi), 1.71 (Moonwater) and 1.90 (Obsidian). Locked is 1.10–1.47 in every theme. Solved survives because it also collapses to a heavier numeral. Locked has no such fallback in Kiln (sunken only) or Moonwater (fog). Tapping a locked cell is specified to do nothing, so Miles gets no feedback at all. *Fix:* Locked = texture plus a ≥3:1 edge treatment in every theme. A tap on a locked cell gets a gentle acknowledgement (mist puff, tile wobble, soft tick) so the input doesn't feel broken.

**[medium]** Large-area luminance swings and busy transitions (DESIGN.md → Transition looks; EXPERIENCE.md → Reduced motion "quick crossfade ~200 ms"). Zen alternates the dark Moonwater (#16242C, L≈0.017) with the light Washi and Kiln (L≈0.77–0.84) every round. Under reduced motion that becomes a 200 ms full-screen dark-to-light switch, which is harsher than the full-motion version. Crystal fracture spins 100–150 high-contrast shard edges, and edges flickering through rotation can produce pattern flicker. *Fix:* Make the reduced-motion crossfade ≥400 ms, or step through a mid-luminance frame. Avoid putting dark and light themes back to back in Zen playlists by default. Cap shard count and edge contrast during rotation, and test every transition with a PEAT/Harding-style flash analyser before shipping.

**[medium]** The tap-target spec is under-defined and too small for kids and Android (DESIGN.md → `tap-min: 44px [ASSUMPTION]`; Layout "minimum tap-min"). 44 is the iOS number, Android asks for 48dp, and research on children's touch input points to larger targets (≥15 mm, about 57pt). The spec doesn't say what is measured (bounding box or inscribed circle). In zero-inset themes (Vector Noir, Washi, `inset-none`), neighbouring cells share an edge with no dead zone, so a fat-finger tap on a boundary picks a neighbour and, in Normal, triggers a power cut. Tier-1 cells are fine at 375pt, and even 360dp: the inscribed circle of a 9B nonagon wedge is about 87pt. But "contains" miniatures at n=7/9 tier-2 shrink to roughly 25pt cells inside a roughly 75pt circle. *Fix:* Define the target as the inscribed circle, ≥48pt, and ≥56pt in kid profile. Treat edge-ambiguous taps (within about 6pt of a shared edge) as no-ops with a hover-style highlight, never as wrong. Make miniatures display-only, and enter a puzzle by tapping its whole container cell.

**[medium]** Normal's punishment level and timer placement work against the anti-math-anxiety stance (EXPERIENCE.md → Modes "Difficulty, not the timer, is the anxiety lever"; State Patterns → Error; Sound & Haptics). One wrong tap brings red, a bass "power-down thud", a heavier haptic, every light on the board dying and the word "power cut" in the design language. Meanwhile the timer *is* the puzzle's outline, so a child can't look at the numbers without watching time drain. Normal "arrives" for Miles after about 10 rounds, with no opt-in specified. *Fix:* Make Normal opt-in, never auto-switched. In kid profiles, drop the thud and the heavy haptic and use a softer non-red cue. Offer the timer as a separate ring outside the shape (setting). Keep round-end flashes to personal bests only, never to slower-than-last-time.

**[medium]** Guide text has no size, can sit on failing backgrounds, and leaves on a timer (DESIGN.md → `typography.guide` "Size not yet specified"; EXPERIENCE.md → Guide text "waits a few seconds, then the hint line replaces it"). Kiln `number` on sand is 4.52:1, passing by 0.02. If the top free space shows the `surface-deep` gradient or vignette, it drops to 3.69:1. A slow 8-year-old reader loses the first line before finishing it (2.2.1). *Fix:* Guide ≥20pt, and ≥24pt at kid distance. Set it on a flat, guaranteed-4.5:1 region. Replace it only after an interaction or ≥8 s, and keep the original line reachable (tap the guide to hear or see it again).

**[medium]** Numeral orientation in radial shapes isn't specified (DESIGN.md → Shapes, 7A/9B wedges, 5B/6A flowers). If numerals rotate to follow wedge orientation, 6 and 9 swap for a child. Avenir and Futura 1/7 at 20 px make it worse. *Fix:* Numerals are always upright to the screen. Pick or tune faces where 6, 9, 1 and 7 are clearly distinct, or underline 6 and 9.

**[low]** The tier-2 operator is near-invisible before unlock (DESIGN.md → Operator cell "25–45% opacity"). Kiln's operator flattened at 25% on tile is about 1.2:1. It counts as an "inactive" element, but it is foreshadowing the player is meant to notice. *Fix:* A minimum of 2:1 with a fog texture, or hide it fully and reveal it as an event.

**[low]** One-handed reach and orientation (Layout → menu in top free space; Open Q22 landscape). On 6.1–6.9" phones the top free space is out of thumb reach, so pausing (the only Normal escape) needs two hands. The web build must not lock orientation (1.3.4). *Fix:* Put the menu button at the bottom, or mirror it bottom-left and bottom-right. Support landscape on web, even if it only letterboxes.

**[low]** No text scaling for words (Voice and Tone; control surface and stats). Custom-drawn UI ignores Dynamic Type and Android font scale (1.4.4). *Fix:* The DOM/native control surface and stats respect the OS text size up to 200% without clipping.

**[low]** Upstream speed signals can leak into calm modes (EXPERIENCE.md Open Q23 "hidden-timer scoring in Zen"; IA → Daily Seed "result card coloured by speed"). A card coloured by speed is colour-only and invites comparison. Hidden Zen timing contradicts "no time pressure in Zen". *Fix:* No speed scoring in Zen, ever. Daily Seed cards encode speed with a shape or label as well as colour, and kid profiles show no speed comparisons.
