# Legal, name and store-listing readiness

## Description

The privacy legal review, name clearance, Tang patent check and store-listing decisions are done before public launch. Every story is hitl.

## Outcome

Numception can launch publicly without a legal or naming blocker; the signal is each check recorded as passed.

## Done when

1. Legal review of COPPA, GDPR-K, the UK Children's Code and Play Families is complete, with any mitigation adopted.
2. The final name passes the clearance checklist and is no longer than 30 characters as a store title.
3. No Tang patent blocks the puzzle format.
4. Store listing and the Play target-audience declaration are decided.

## Boundaries

Decisions and reviews by people. Not engineering work, which goes to the owning epic.
