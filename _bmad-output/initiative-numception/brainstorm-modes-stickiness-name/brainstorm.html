The core
Every solve changes the light of a self-similar structure at every scale.
synthesis by coach · from the user's “defining core: the inception zoom and the solve transition”
 
1Zoom and transition are one idea. Combo glow, power grid, fog reveal, the wrong-tap power cut, the surprise glitch, the climb and the pyramid are that idea at different scales.
2State as light. Geometry is the HUD, so Zen and Normal are mostly palette and intensity. Two modes come cheap.
3Two engines carry everything. Generator + uniqueness checker (seeds, daily, ghosts, teacher, Library, Operator Sudoku, top-down) and the rating model. The POC builds both.
4Surprise at mastery replaces levels and the unlock tree as the progression system, and fits no-pay-to-win.
◆ Chosen directionPOC = classic round + tower round with the zoom and transition at the centre, Zen + Normal, a +/− generator with uniqueness, and rating logs. v1 and Later follow the accepted phase plan.
